About LibraryTrack

A shared shelf
for every library.

LibraryTrack keeps one clean catalogue across a whole network of libraries, so a title lives in one place and every library tracks its own copies.

Find anything

Search by title, author, ISBN, genre, or library, and filter down to what's on the shelf right now.

Borrow with ease

Request a loan, join a waitlist when a copy is out, and get notified the moment it's free again.

Give back

Donate books you've finished with, or suggest titles you'd love to see the library carry.

How it works

Every book lives in the catalogue once, tied to its ISBN, so there are no duplicate records to keep in sync across the network. Each library keeps its own inventory on top of that shared record, tracking how many copies it holds and how many are free right now. Members search the whole network from one place, borrow from whichever library has a copy, and join a waitlist when everything is out. Each library's admin looks after only their own shelves: approving loans, reviewing donations and purchase suggestions, and keeping stock accurate.
